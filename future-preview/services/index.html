Architectural Services Structured for Clarity

We guide homeowners through every phase of building and renovating—from evaluating raw land or existing structures to navigating town boards, producing flawless construction drawings, and observing the build.

Phase 1 • Pre-Design & Discovery

Site Feasibility & Zoning Review

Before purchasing property or finalizing a project scope, we perform rigorous code analysis to uncover hidden building restrictions, setback constraints, wetland buffers, and allowable Floor Area Ratios (FAR).

The Client Challenge

Purchasing land or planning an addition without knowing if the local municipality will permit the desired square footage, height, or driveway access.

What KOA Does

Audits municipal master plans, topographical surveys, wetland maps, and deed restrictions to map out the exact allowable building envelope.

Client Receives: Comprehensive Zoning & Feasibility Report, 3D Buildable Envelope Diagram, and Municipal Approval Strategy.

Phase 2 • Conceptual & Schematic Design

Custom Residential Design & Alterations

Crafting tailored residences that balance modern spatial drama with functional daily living, natural lighting, energy efficiency, and long-term durability.

The Client Challenge

Translating lifestyle desires into coherent floorplans and elevations that respect the site's natural topography and neighborhood context.

What KOA Does

Develops iterative floorplans, exterior massing options, and sun-angle studies, testing multiple design directions collaboratively.

Client Receives: Schematic Floor Plans, Exterior Elevations, Section Studies, and Material Palette Boards.

Phase 3 • Visual Verification

Photorealistic 3D & Virtual Reality

We bridge the gap between 2D blueprints and real-world reality. Immersive 1:1 scale VR walkthroughs allow you to experience rooms, window vistas, and finishes before breaking ground.

The Client Challenge

Difficulty visualizing flat floorplans, leading to costly change orders during framing and construction.

What KOA Does

Builds true-to-life 3D digital twins with precise lighting, material reflections, and seasonal solar orientation.

Client Receives: 4K Exterior/Interior Renderings, Interactive VR Headset Sessions, and 360° Panorama Tours.

Active Capability • In-House Rendering Studio
Phase 4 • Regulatory Entitlements

Municipal & Review Board Representation

Representing your project with authority before Planning Boards, Zoning Boards of Adjustment (ZBA), Historic Commissions, and Architectural Review Boards across NY, NJ, and CT.

The Client Challenge

Board hearings can delay projects for months or result in costly denials without compelling visual and legal code justification.

What KOA Does

Prepares authoritative visual impact packages, sightline studies, hardship justifications, and delivers professional testimony.

Client Receives: Board Presentation Packages, Sightline Analysis, Variance Application Sets, and Hearing Attendance.

Phase 5 • Technical Execution

Construction Documents & Consultant Coordination

Comprehensive, airtight drawing sets that coordinate structural engineering, MEP (mechanical, electrical, plumbing), civil engineering, and specialized millwork detailing.

The Client Challenge

Vague or uncoordinated drawings lead to contractor disputes, unforeseen structural clashes, and budget inflation on site.

What KOA Does

Directs and integrates all specialist engineers into a single, cohesive architectural drawing set with explicit detailing.

Client Receives: Complete Permit & Construction Drawing Set, Engineering Coordination Overlay, and Specifications.

Phase 6 • Bidding & Construction Oversight

Contractor Bidding Support & Site Observation

We assist clients in vetting general contractors, leveling competitive bids, and conducting regular job-site observations to verify that construction matches the design intent.

The Client Challenge

Comparing apples to oranges across contractor estimates, and lacking technical oversight when deviations happen in the field.

What KOA Does

Creates standardized bid sheets, answers contractor RFIs (Requests for Information), reviews shop drawings, and performs periodic site inspections.

Client Receives: Bid Analysis Spreadsheet, Submittal Reviews, Field Observation Reports, and Punch-List Verification.
