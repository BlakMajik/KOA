A Guided, Transparent Roadmap

How We Bring Your Vision to Life

Hiring an architect should not feel intimidating. We have broken the complex journey of designing, permitting, and building a custom home into 11 clear, predictable milestones.

01
Phase 1 • Initial Contact

Initial Inquiry & Project Fit

You share your property location, general project goals, desired timeline, and approximate construction budget via our qualification intake. We perform an immediate preliminary check to confirm alignment.

15-Min Fit Assessment Preliminary Code Check
02
Phase 1 • Discovery

Discovery Consultation & Goals Alignment

An in-depth working session (in-person or video) where we dive into how your family lives, architectural preferences, spatial wishlists, and aesthetic inspiration.

Detailed Project Brief Spatial Program Inventory
03
Phase 1 • Due Diligence

Site & Feasibility Review

We analyze property surveys, local zoning codes, environmental buffers (wetlands, steep slopes), utility connections, and solar orientation to define the buildable parameters.

Zoning Envelope Diagram FAR & Setback Audit
04
Phase 1 • Agreement

Scope of Work & Proposal

A formal proposal outlining phased deliverables, project schedule milestones, estimated consultant requirements, and transparent professional fee structures.

AIA-Standard Contract [Placeholder] Phase Milestone Schedule
05
Phase 2 • Design

Concept & Schematic Design

We generate initial floorplans, exterior massing studies, and spatial arrangements. We iterate collaboratively with you until the primary layout and architectural form feel perfect.

Schematic Floorplans Exterior Massing Studies Material Concepts
06
Phase 2 • Verification

3D Visualization & Client Decisions

Using our in-house 3D rendering and Virtual Reality technology, you virtually step inside your home. You evaluate ceiling heights, light penetration, and material textures before any construction commits.

Photorealistic 4K Renders Immersive VR Walkthrough Solar & Shadow Analysis
07
Phase 3 • Technical Development

Design Development & Consultant Coordination

We integrate structural engineers, MEP engineers, civil/septic designers, and landscape architects into our central building model to eliminate unforeseen on-site clashes.

Structural Integration MEP System Schematics Window & Door Schedules
08
Phase 3 • Entitlements

Municipal & Review Board Approvals

We prepare the formal variance applications, architectural review submittals, and visual evidence sets, and represent your project before town boards to secure all needed permits.

ZBA / Planning Board Testimony Historic Commission Filings Building Permit Approvals
09
Phase 4 • Documentation & Procurement

Construction Documentation & Contractor Bidding

We produce an airtight, fully detailed drawing and specification set. We assist you in distributing the package to qualified general contractors and leveling their bids to ensure fair pricing.

Full Construction Blueprints Architectural Specifications Bid Leveling Matrix
10
Phase 5 • Construction Phase

Construction Administration & Site Oversight

During construction, we conduct regular site visits, review shop drawings, answer contractor RFIs, review payment applications, and protect design fidelity in the field.

Periodic Site Visits Field Observation Reports RFI & Submittal Processing
11
Phase 6 • Final Delivery

Completion, Punch List & Move-In

We perform the final walkthrough to generate a comprehensive punch list, verify that all mechanical warranties and closeout documents are received, and celebrate your move-in.

Final Punch List Inspection Certificate of Occupancy Support Project Closeout Record
