The Designs by KOA Difference

Architecture Grounded in Problem-Solving

Exceptional architecture is not merely about how a space looks—it is about navigating complex zoning regulations, solving site constraints, protecting your budget, and orchestrating construction without surprises.

📐

Zoning & Regulatory Navigation

We dissect municipal bylaws, Floor Area Ratios (FAR), setback restrictions, steep slope ordinances, and coastal flood zones before a single line is finalized.

  • Zoning variance strategy & filings
  • Planning & Architectural Review Board representation
  • FEMA flood zone & coastal CAM compliance
👓

3D Virtual Reality Decision-Making

Walk through your home in photorealistic 1:1 scale before construction starts. Eliminate guesswork on daylight, ceiling heights, sightlines, and finishes.

  • Interactive VR walkthroughs for clients
  • Daylight & solar angle shadow studies
  • Board of adjustment visual evidence packages
🏗️

Construction-Phase Oversight

We don’t abandon you at the permit phase. We coordinate with structural engineers, review contractor submittals, and conduct on-site observation through completion.

  • Contractor bidding & cost evaluation
  • Site observation & field reports
  • Direct engineering consultant coordination
Service Area & Jurisdictions

Serving New York, New Jersey & Connecticut

Our practice is designed around the unique architectural character, municipal codes, and environmental landscapes of the Tri-State region.

New York

NY

From Manhattan brownstones and loft renovations to Hudson Valley hillside estates and Westchester expansions.

  • NYC DOB & Landmarks Preservation (LPC)
  • Westchester & Hudson Valley steep slopes
  • Hamptons & Long Island conservation districts

New Jersey

NJ

Serving Bergen, Morris, Essex, Somerset, and Monmouth counties for luxury new builds and expansive additions.

  • Municipal Board of Adjustment representation
  • Historic district and tree preservation compliance
  • Stormwater management & impervious cover audits

Connecticut

CT

Focusing on Fairfield County, Greenwich, Westport, New Canaan, and coastal Long Island Sound properties.

  • Coastal Area Management (CAM) Act permitting
  • Inland Wetlands & Watercourses representation
  • Greenwich Historic District Commission reviews
