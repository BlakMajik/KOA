Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our file handoffs, revision cycles, and fair-value guarantees.

We work with all standard architectural software formats, including Revit (.rvt), AutoCAD (.dwg), SketchUp (.skp), Rhino (.3dm), ArchiCAD, FBX, OBJ, and 3ds Max files. Even if you only have 2D PDF blueprints and hand sketches with elevations, our 3D modelers can construct the geometry from scratch.
Our structured 3-phase delivery guarantees you never waste time: First, we send a Clay / Camera Angle pass so you can approve compositions and focal lengths. Second, we submit a full-texture and lighting draft for your feedback. Third, we implement all material, color, and entourage adjustments before final high-resolution delivery.
In 3D visualization, roughly 60% of the total labor is creating the 3D model, setting up materials, and dialing in environment lighting. Once your digital building exists, rendering an additional camera angle takes significantly less time. We pass those savings directly back to you instead of double-billing.
We operate on a standard 50% deposit to commence 3D modeling, with the remaining 50% balance due only after you have reviewed and approved your watermarked proof. Full commercial rights and unwatermarked 4K/8K files are released immediately upon final balance.
